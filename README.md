# TaskConnectTaskConnect
TaskConnect is a dynamic and intuitive task marketplace platform designed to seamlessly connect clients with skilled service providers, referred to as "Taskers." Whether you need a professional chef, a babysitter, or help with website development, TaskConnect simplifies the process of finding, booking, and managing services.

Key Features
1. Effortless Service Request & Booking
Clients can easily search for a wide range of services, such as "Chef," "Baby Sitting," or "Website Development." Once a desired service is found, clients can book the service directly through the app.

Search Functionality: Quickly find services using the search bar.

Category Browsing: Explore services organized into clear categories (e.g., All, Baby Sitting, Chef, Cleaning).

Booking Management: Submit service requests and await approval or rejection from the Tasker.

2. Tasker Management & Communication
Taskers receive client requests and have the flexibility to approve or reject them based on their availability and preferences.

Request Notifications: Taskers are notified of new service requests.

Approve/Reject Requests: Taskers can accept or decline job offers.

Tasker Dashboard: A dedicated dashboard for Taskers to view their completion rate, response rate, and recent jobs. Quick actions allow them to view requests, check ratings, manage their profile, and access support.

3. Ella - Your AI Assistant
TaskConnect features "Ella," a friendly AI assistant ready to answer any questions you might have about using the app, booking services, or managing payments.

Instant Support: Get quick answers to common queries about the app's functionalities.

Payment Guidance: Ella can guide you through the payment process, including M-PESA STK Push transactions and escrow details.

4. Seamless Tasker Onboarding
TaskConnect provides a robust onboarding process for new Taskers, ensuring a secure and verified community of service providers.

Profile Creation: Taskers can create and manage their profiles, including personal information, contact details, and a bio.

ID Verification: Securely submit essential identification documents such as KRA PIN and ID Number.

Service Listing: Taskers can list the services they offer, specify their rates, and define their areas of service.

Document Uploads: Supporting documents can be uploaded for verification.

Admin Approval: Submitted Tasker profiles and documents are reviewed and approved or rejected by the admin to maintain quality and trust within the platform.
